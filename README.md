The U.S. Department of the Treasury manages federal finances, public debt, Treasury securities, U.S. currency production, tax administration, financial sanctions, and economic-statistical reporting. Treasury bureaus publish several public APIs, anchored by the Bureau of the Fiscal Service's Fiscal Data API and the Office of Foreign Assets Control's Sanctions List Service.

**Areas:** Federal Government · Finance · Debt · Sanctions

- [IRS Public APIs](https://apis.io/apis/department-of-the-treasury/irs-public-apis/) — The Internal Revenue Service exposes select public datasets and tools through download endpoints, including Tax-Exempt Organization Search.

- [Department of the Treasury Debt API](https://apis.io/apis/department-of-the-treasury/department-of-the-treasury-debt-api/) — Federal debt-related datasets
- [Department of the Treasury Exchange Rates API](https://apis.io/apis/department-of-the-treasury/department-of-the-treasury-exchange-rates-api/) — Foreign currency exchange rates
- [Department of the Treasury Interest Rates API](https://apis.io/apis/department-of-the-treasury/department-of-the-treasury-interest-rates-api/) — Average interest rates and yield curves
- [Department of the Treasury Sanctions Lists API](https://apis.io/apis/department-of-the-treasury/department-of-the-treasury-sanctions-lists-api/) — SDN and Consolidated Sanctions list downloads
- [Department of the Treasury Search API](https://apis.io/apis/department-of-the-treasury/department-of-the-treasury-search-api/) — Structured search across the SDN and Consolidated lists
- [Department of the Treasury Spending API](https://apis.io/apis/department-of-the-treasury/department-of-the-treasury-spending-api/) — Federal spending datasets
- [Department of the Treasury Operations API](https://apis.io/apis/department-of-the-treasury/department-of-the-treasury-treasury-operations-api/) — Daily and monthly Treasury statements

## How this profile is made
